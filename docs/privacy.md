# LangarSeva Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

LangarSeva ("the app") helps people find free community kitchens (langars) near them. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data we collect

| Data | When | Why | Stored? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Approximate / precise location | While the app is open and you allow it | To show langars near you and sort them by distance | No. Location is sent to our server only as part of a search query and is not saved. |
| Phone number or Google/Apple account email and name | Only if you sign in | To create your account so you can add langars, join seva slots and save favourites | Yes, in your profile until you delete your account. |
| Langar details and photos you submit | When you add a langar | To show the langar to other users after review | Yes, publicly, as part of the langar listing. |
| Seva signups, favourites, reports | When you use those features | To provide those features | Yes, linked to your account. |

We do **not** collect advertising identifiers, contacts, or analytics, and we do not sell data to anyone.

## Where data is stored
Our backend is Supabase (hosted on AWS, Mumbai region). Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

## Third parties
- **Google Maps**: map tiles and geocoding are provided by Google and are subject to Google's privacy policy.
- **Google / Apple sign-in**: used only to authenticate you if you choose those options.
- **UPI / payment apps**: tapping "Donate" opens your own payment app. We never see or process payments.

## Your choices
- Browsing langars never requires an account or location permission (you can search by city).
- You can delete your account at any time from **Profile → Delete account**. This permanently removes your profile, seva signups, favourites and reports. Langars you contributed remain on the map without any link to you.

## Children
The app is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Contact
Questions or deletion requests: **support@langarseva.app**

## Changes
We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
