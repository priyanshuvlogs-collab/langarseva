# Google Play listing (English, India)

**App name (30):** LangarSeva – Langar Near Me

**Short description (80):**
Find free langar (community kitchens) near you, see timings, volunteer & donate.

**Full description (4000):**
LangarSeva shows every langar around you on a map, so no one has to search for a free, warm meal.

🍛 Langars near you – open the app and instantly see langars and gurudwaras serving free food, sorted by distance.
🕒 Open now – check today's timings and filter to only the langars serving right now.
🧭 Directions in one tap – navigate with Google Maps, or call the langar directly.
🙏 Seva – see where volunteers are needed and join a seva slot (roti making, serving, cleaning).
💛 Donate – support a langar through its UPI ID or website. Payments go directly to the organisers; LangarSeva takes nothing.
➕ Add a langar – know a langar that's missing? Add it with photos and timings. Our volunteers review every submission before it appears.
🌐 Available in English, हिन्दी and ਪੰਜਾਬੀ.

No account is needed to browse. Sign in only if you want to add a langar, join seva or save favourites.

Langar is the Sikh tradition of a free community kitchen open to all, regardless of religion, caste or background. LangarSeva is a community project to make that generosity easy to find.
